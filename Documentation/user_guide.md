## Usage
#### Getting Started
Upon opening the app, you will be greeted by an empty home page. At the top, you will see a name "John Doe", which you will be able to change in the settings page. Next to your name is the notification icon. When you have any due dates tomorrow, notifications will appear here. If you are on android, notifications will also be pushed to your phone.
To navigate to settings to change your name, please use the hamburger menu at the top left.
#### Adding, Deleting, and Editing Terms
You can add as many terms as you need to organize your courses. To add a term, simply click the "Add Term" button at the bottom of the home screen.
You can delete terms by either swiping left on the term and clicking the trash icon (Android), or by selecting any item in the list and then right-clicking and selecting delete (Windows).
To edit the Term's title, start or end dates, or to toggle notifications, you will need to navigate to the Term Details Page. To do this, select the term you wish to edit and click the "View/Edit" button that appears at the bottom of the screen.
At the top of the Term Details Page, you will see the term's title. Click on this to edit the title. To the right of that, you will see the start and end dates. Click on these to edit them. Finally, you will see a toggle for notifications. If you want to be notified of important dates, make sure this is toggled on.
Be sure to click the "Save" button at the bottom of the screen to save your changes.
### Adding, Deleting, and Editing Courses
To add a course, navigate to the Term Details Page and click the "Add Course" button at the bottom of the screen. 
To delete a course, swipe left on the course and click the trash icon (Android), or select the course and right-click to delete (Windows).
To edit a course or view more information, you must navigate to the course's details page. Select the course and click the "View/Edit" button that appears at the bottom.
Here you can edit the course's title, start and end dates, and toggle notifications just as in the Term Details Page.
Additionally, you can add course notes, choose a course status, and instructor name, email, and phone number. Click on the share button to share your course information.
Click the share button beneath the course notes to share or save notes to your device.
Be sure to click the "Save" button at the bottom of the screen to save your changes.
Every course has a Performance Assessment and Objective Assessment. These cannot be deleted, but can be marked as complete. To view or edit assessment information, select an assessment and click the "View/Edit" button that appears at the bottom of the screen.
### Viewing and Editing Assessments
On the assessment details page, you can edit the assessment title, start date, due date, and toggle notifications. You can also change the status of the assessment.
Be sure to click the "Save" button at the bottom of the screen to save your changes.
### View Progress Report on Course and Assessment Completion
To view your progress report, navigate back to the home page and from the hamburger menu at the top left navigate to the "Academic Progress" page.
Here, you can view information about your overall course completion status and a list of all courses with their status and completion dates, if applicable.
Clicking the toggle button at the top of the report page will similarly show you progress and status of all assessments.
